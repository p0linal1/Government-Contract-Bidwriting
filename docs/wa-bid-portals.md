## Where Doug should register, in order

1. **WEBS**, free. It covers the courts office, Corrections, and other state justice agencies, plus some local governments.
2. **Public Purchase.** It covers Spokane County, Yakima County, Bellevue, and Kennewick. Kennewick runs the BiPIN network, so this is his Tri-Cities home market.
3. **OpenGov.** It covers Seattle and Pierce County.
4. **King County's portal and ProcureWare** (Snohomish County and the City of Spokane) each need their own account. The **MRSC roster** is required by Benton County and the City of Spokane, and many small cities use it.

Clark, Thurston, Kitsap, Whatcom, and Benton counties post on their own websites, so the agent can read those without any account.
