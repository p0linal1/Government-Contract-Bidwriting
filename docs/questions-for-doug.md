## To qualify a bid

These decide pursue or pass. Without them, many RFPs stop at "unknown".

1. **Value range.** Does $1 million to $5 million mean the total over every year and option, or per year?
2. **Military.** Does "no military" also rule out ordinary IT work for a Department of Defense office, such as a case system for a base legal office? And is Veterans Affairs fine?
3. **Set-asides.** Public records show Cadocary is small and holds no SBA certifications, so only small business set-asides apply. Is that right, and do you plan to apply for any?
4. **Insurance.** For each one you carry, what is the limit, carrier, and expiry: general liability, professional (errors and omissions), cyber, auto, and workers' comp? Can a buyer be added as additional insured?
5. **Certifications.** Which do you hold, or have applied for: Washington OMWBE (minority- or woman-owned), SOC 2, or any other?
6. **CJIS.** Do you have written CJIS security policies, and can everyone who would touch the data pass an FBI fingerprint background check?
7. **Hosting.** If a buyer needs a hosted system, where would it run (for example AWS GovCloud or Azure Government)?
8. **Other registrations.** Are you registered on Public Purchase, and when does SAM.gov expire? Your SAM.gov registration lists codes like janitorial and landscaping. Do you want to keep those? The license number is known (605 623 739), and WEBS registration was started Oct 26.

## To draft a bid

These are what a proposal claims. Without them, the drafter leaves marked blanks.

9. **Past government contracts.** Public records show none. Is that right? List any, including subcontracts: buyer, years, dollar value, and one line on the work. Which may we name?
10. **Product users.** How many public defense offices or attorneys use DocketBot and ClientCheck today, and may we name any? Do both still work for courts already on the new statewide court system, such as Tacoma Municipal Court?
11. **Client projects.** For SpendLogic, Hotels4Truckers, and PURLPal: what years, roughly what contract value, and may a government bid name the client?
12. **References.** Your capability statement lists a contact at SpendLogic, Hotels4Truckers, and PURLPal. Has each agreed to be a named reference, and who else, especially at a government buyer, will speak for you?
13. **Staff.** Who would work on a bid: name, role, years of experience, and hours a week available? Can we have each resume?
14. **Signer.** You are the only governor on the state record. May anyone else sign bids and sworn forms?
15. **Years in business.** The state record says Cadocary was formed October 10, 2024. Is there earlier work, under another name, that counts toward experience?

## To price a bid

16. **Rates.** What is your hourly rate for each role?
17. **Price list.** What do you charge for licenses, setup, data migration, training, hosting, and yearly support?
18. **Floor.** What is the lowest total you would accept for a contract in your range?
19. **Terms.** What payment terms and discounts do you usually offer?
20. **Seat pricing.** Do DocketBot and ClientCheck stay $30 a month per user for a government office, or is there a site license?
