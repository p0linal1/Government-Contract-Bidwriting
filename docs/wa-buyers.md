# Washington justice buyers: who runs what

Gathered October 28, 2026 from council and commission records, budgets, bid pages, and vendor announcements. "Inferred" marks a conclusion drawn from the sources rather than stated in them. Pierce and Spokane counties block automated reads, so their entries come from search-engine copies and are thinner.

## What it means for Doug

- **Most of his lane is under his $1 million floor.** Prosecutor and defender systems in mid-size counties cost $80,000 to $500,000 over several years. Only jail systems and the biggest counties pass $1 million. Whether the floor means the total over all years or per year (question C1) decides how much of the market is open to him.
- **Karpel wins prosecutor deals.** It won every recent Washington prosecutor system that closed: Kitsap, Whatcom, and Lewis, plus King County's criminal system without a bid. Journal Technologies is the usual runner-up.
- **Few bidders.** Each recent deal drew zero to two proposals. Pend Oreille County's prosecutor RFP drew none, which suggests small offices are underserved (inferred).
- **Price is a small part of the score.** Kitsap weighted cost at 25 of 100, and King County gives price 22 of 130 and demos 30. Fit, moving the old data, and demos decide most deals.
- **Public defense is the opening, not prosecutors.** Prosecutors leaving JustWare mostly go to Pine Technologies, a company started by former JustWare staff, at about $85,000 a year. Public defense offices can use state grant money for defender-only systems.

## JustWare

- The vendor, Journal Technologies, ended JustWare support on June 30, 2021. Customers could stay only while moving to its newer system, and hosted customers could move to an outside host. Source: a 2022 contract amendment with Weber County, Utah, https://webercountyutah.gov/commission/documents/uploads/F9%20%20Journal%20JustWare.pdf
- "Hosting ends in 2027" comes only from Snohomish County's 2026 papers, which don't say whose hosting ends.
- Washington offices found on it: Snohomish County Prosecutor (leaving for Pine by July 1, 2027), Yakima County Prosecutor and Assigned Counsel (possibly still on it), and Federal Way Municipal Court (unknown). Already moved: Okanogan County Prosecutor (to Karpel, about 2025, inferred), City of Spokane (to Journal's newer system, 2021 to 2022), Federal Way City Prosecutor (to Karpel, 2023).
